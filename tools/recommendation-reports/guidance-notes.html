Guidance Notes

Practical points to check when preparing and submitting UHD recommendation reports.

Approval & pre-meet requirements

If the report requires approval by Deputy or Chief Finance Officer, Finance Performance Committee and Board of Directors, a pre-meet may be required with the author, Category Manager and HoP prior to submitting through ImageNow.

Reports under £60k

  • If under £60k but fully compliant and includes full life cycle costs and winning bid, the Category Manager can approve the summary report.
  • If not compliant but under £60k, this will need to be approved by HoP.
  • If under £60k but fully compliant, no report is needed, but a full audit trail must be available on request at any time and kept in line with retention policy etc.

Framework Agreements (FA)

Please ensure the correct templates have been used. If it is not a mini competition, it will require CFO approval.

Figures, VAT & formatting

  • All figures for approving committees will now be based on inclusive of VAT and should always be stated as this. The OJEU value excludes VAT, but wording has been adjusted to cover this difference in thresholds.
  • Please do not use decimals. Use the £ sign and commas for thousands, and use Calibri 11.
  • Please ensure all cost pressure figures are highlighted in red, with brackets in red and bold, for example (£1,000 PA).

Risks, conflicts & benchmarking

Please ensure you have checked this does not impact national strategies such as NCP, VCM or CSR plans. Also consider whether you are comfortable that the NHS Spend Comparison service has been used to benchmark these agreements.

Using the latest template & telling the story

  • Please only use the latest template and remove DRAFT. Ensure consistency and avoid unnecessary repetition.
  • Please do not rely on the template to tell your story. If something did not happen, do not state that it did — for example evaluations, in accordance with SFIs and EU regs, post tender etc. Make no assumptions and know the audience who will approve the final report.

Total contract value & equipment

Include the whole life of the contract in the total contract value — contract term plus extensions if applicable. If equipment is involved, ensure the appendix is clear regarding warranty.

Business case, new services & financial visibility

  • If the final recommendation does not reconcile back to the approved business case, further approval will need to be sought and the Finance Business Partner (FBP) and Directorate Manager (DM) made aware.
  • If this is a new service and there are no current costs, state the figure/funds approved so that if the recommendation is lower, the cost avoidance matches and this is made clear.
  • Please ensure the FBP and DM names are included so final approvers are aware they have had visibility and the FBP has validated costs/supports recommendations.

Terms & Conditions and framework checks

Ensure all T&Cs have been checked and all framework points addressed. Responsibility for this sits with us and not the framework provider. We would be liable if the contract award notice had not been reviewed and the Trust/specification were not covered.

Recommendation not awarding to the 1st placed tender

Please seek guidance from the Category Manager regarding a recommendation that does not award to the 1st placed tender as per award criteria, as this may be rejected and not supported.

Reports going to DCFO / CFO / FPC / BoD

For all reports going to DCFO/CFO/FPC and BoD, please ensure the scoring summary and commercial summary have been validated by the FBP and are clear. This will be checked and may be rejected if not.

Agreement Approvals & NDAs

  • Agreement Approvals should only be for an agreement already covered by a previous report.
  • For completion of Non-Disclosure Agreements, please liaise with the Healthcare Service Contracts Coordinator/Paralegal. This will require sign-off by at least Grade 8a.

Key reminders

  • Use the latest template.
  • State committee figures inclusive of VAT.
  • Use whole-life contract value.
  • Include FBP and DM visibility where required.
  • Keep the audit trail available.

Approver abbreviations

HoP — Head of Procurement

FBP — Finance Business Partner

DM — Directorate Manager

FPC — Finance Performance Committee

BoD — Board of Directors
