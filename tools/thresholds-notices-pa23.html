Thresholds & Notices (PA23)

Identify the applicable Schedule 1 threshold, test whether the procurement is above threshold, and surface potential PA23 notice requirements based on procurement route and lifecycle triggers.

Controlled version: thresholds current for 1 January 2026–31 December 2027; guidance reviewed 21 August 2026. This is a decision-support tool, not legal advice. Always confirm the latest rules and procedure-specific guidance before acting.
Assessment

Start the PA23 assessment

All threshold values in this tool are inclusive of VAT.

The revised flow first checks whether PA23 is in scope, then identifies the authority and contract category, applies the correct commencement-date threshold, compares the estimated contract value, and only then considers route and notice triggers.

For threshold purposes, NHS Trusts and NHS Foundation Trusts — including UHD — are treated as central government authorities.

What is the estimated contract value?
